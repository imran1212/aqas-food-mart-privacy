# Privacy Policy — AQAS Food Mart

**Effective date:** 23 October 2026  
**App name:** AQAS Food Mart  
**Developer/Service provider:** AQAS Food Mart

> **Contact:** smartvoucherpk@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

AQAS Food Mart (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the AQAS Food Mart mobile application.

The app is designed to let customers browse food products, including achar (pickles), chutneys, and murabba, manage a shopping cart, and place orders for delivery where the service is available.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

- **Account information:** information associated with your sign-in account, such as your name, email address, and account identifier. If Google Sign-In is enabled and used, authentication information made available through that process.
- **Order and delivery information:** customer name, Pakistani mobile number, city, area, complete delivery address, products ordered, pack sizes, quantities, prices, order totals, payment method, order status, and optional order notes.
- **Support information:** information you choose to provide when contacting us about an order, delivery, or app issue.
- **Technical information:** limited technical information that the app or service providers process to operate the app, maintain security, authenticate users, and troubleshoot errors. The information actually processed depends on the services and features enabled.

Please do not enter passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information into optional order notes.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:
- Create or manage accounts and authenticate users.
- Display products, available pack sizes, and prices.
- Process and confirm Cash on Delivery (COD) orders.
- Arrange delivery and contact customers about orders.
- Display order history and order status.
- Respond to support requests.
- Protect the service against fraud, misuse, and unauthorised access.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

We do not use delivery details for unrelated marketing purposes unless we have an appropriate legal basis and, where required, consent.

## 4. Payments

The app is intended to support Cash on Delivery. Payment is collected according to the delivery arrangements provided by the seller. Online payment processing is not described as an available feature in this policy. If online payments are introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant provider and information handling.

## 5. Storage and Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, as part of its intended account and order functionality. Information processed through these services may be stored on infrastructure operated by Google or its service providers, subject to their applicable terms and privacy documentation.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers or other parties when reasonably necessary to operate the service, fulfil orders, protect users, comply with law, or with your direction or consent.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, fulfil orders, provide support, resolve disputes, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Order and transaction records may need to be retained for legitimate business, accounting, or legal purposes even after an account deletion request. Retention depends on the type of information and the reason it is retained.

## 7. Account and Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing **smartvoucherpk@gmail.com** with the subject line “AQAS Food Mart — Account and Data Deletion Request.” Provide enough information for us to identify your account, but do not send your password.

We will review the request and delete eligible personal information where required and legally permissible. Certain order records may be retained where necessary for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. A request does not mean deletion has already occurred; we may verify the request before acting.

## 8. Data Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect access to your device and account and contact us if you believe your account has been used without authorisation.

## 9. Children's Privacy

AQAS Food Mart is a shopping app and is not specifically directed at children. Children should use the app with appropriate parent or guardian involvement where required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us using the email below so we can review the situation.

## 10. International Processing

Depending on the infrastructure used by our service providers, information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own. Where applicable, such processing is subject to provider terms and applicable legal requirements.

## 11. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You can choose not to provide certain information, but we may then be unable to authenticate your account, process your order, or deliver products.
